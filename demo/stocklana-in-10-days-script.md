# Video: "Stocklana in 10 days" (≈3:00, @expertvagabond, follow-up to "Before you build on Solana")

Voice: first person, plain, specific. Footage: the stockcurve console, the tracks row, the launch page, Clawpump's
Meteora launcher, the sponsor reply, the checklist table. Numbers spelled out for TTS.

**0:00** "This is the ten-day version of the Stocklana hackathon, in the order I'd actually use. I did all of it once; I just
did some of it on the wrong day."

**0:15 · day zero and one** "Day zero is the identity afternoon: mailbox, name, handles, logo, repo, sponsor accounts, keys
made inside the dashboards, a wallet with a one-SOL floor. Day one you don't build. You scan every submission so far and
write one sentence per track: the thing the judge will click. Six sentences. That file runs the rest of the week."

**0:45 · day two** "Day two is six launches, the simplest form of each. A pool quoted in a stock on Meteora. An agent token
paired with a Sunrise stock, launched from Clawpump's own dashboard, which already does Meteora. A curve anchored to a
PreStocks mark, one to a Tessera mark, one on the Pyth feeds your key actually returns. Under a third of a SOL. Six links
that resolve."

**1:15 · day three** "Day three is the live page. Every pool, its reference, its basis, and the six-track row. Deploy it, then
reply on every sponsor's hackathon post with the link. A sponsor's account answered ours in twelve minutes and described the
stack correctly. That sentence is in the submission now."

**1:40 · days four and five** "Day four is the product: a page where a wallet signs one transaction to launch a pool on your
config, and one real-size config so it isn't a toy. Day five is the keeper that migrates, bands, and sweeps fees on a
schedule, and the agent identity on Clawpump: verified wallet, prospectus, marketplace, a paid endpoint."

**2:10 · day six** "Day six you submit. With four days left. Then you keep editing behind the link."

**2:20 · days seven to nine** "Day seven is one real launch with distribution: the post, the sponsor threads, the people who
already watch launches. Bots fill about one SOL and stop; the rest is humans finding it. Day eight is the video, after every
number in it has been read back from chain. Day nine is follow-ups: entitlements, key links, the demo script for the
room you'll be in next."

**2:45 · day ten and close** "Day ten is the buffer, and you'll use it. What this order skips: thirteen demo pools, trading
with the project wallet, rebuilding a button the sponsor already shipped, and cutting the video before the six rows exist.
The table's in the repo."
