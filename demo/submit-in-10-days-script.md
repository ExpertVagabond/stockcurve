# Video: "How to submit a project on Solana in 10 days" (≈3:00, @expertvagabond, second in the series)

Voice: first person, plain, one day per beat. Device on the left: a ten-day calendar strip; each day lights up with its
deliverable and its "done means." Right: day badge, the deliverable in one line, the done-means line. Real marks: Solana,
Solscan, GitHub, X, Phantom. Numbers spelled out for TTS.

**0:00 · open** "How to submit a project on Solana in ten days. One deliverable a day. Each day ends with something a
stranger can click, and if it isn't done, the next day doesn't start."

**0:15 · day zero** "Day zero is identity. Mailbox on your domain, name and handles, logo set, an empty repo, every sponsor
account and key made from inside its dashboard, and one wallet with a one-SOL floor. Done means one login for everything."

**0:35 · day one** "Day one, you don't build. Read every sponsor's pinned post and dashboard, scan what's been submitted, and
write one sentence per track: the artifact a judge will click. Six sentences. That file runs the week."

**0:55 · day two** "Day two is six launches. The simplest form of each sentence, on mainnet, using the sponsor's own tool
where one exists. Done means six links that resolve."

**1:10 · day three** "Day three is the live page: every artifact and its status on one URL. Then reply on each sponsor's
hackathon post with that URL. They answer, sometimes in minutes, and their words go in your submission."

**1:30 · day four** "Day four is the product surface: the one thing a user does without you. A page where a wallet signs one
transaction. And one real-size config, so it isn't a toy."

**1:45 · day five** "Day five is what runs while you sleep: the keeper on a schedule, the fee sweep, and the agent's on-chain
identity if there is one."

**2:00 · day six** "Day six, you submit. Four days early. Paste-ready text, every track ticked, links only to things that
resolve. Then keep editing behind the link."

**2:15 · days seven to nine** "Day seven is one real launch with distribution: sized to what can actually be filled,
announced from the project handle, in the sponsor threads. Day eight is the video, after every number is read back from
chain. Day nine is follow-ups: entitlements, key links, the demo script for the next room."

**2:40 · day ten and close** "Day ten is the buffer. You'll use it. What this order skips: demo pools by the dozen, trading
with the project wallet, rebuilding a button the sponsor already shipped, and cutting the video before the six sentences
exist. Identity, six sentences, six launches, live page, product, automation, submit, one real launch, video, follow-ups,
buffer. Ten days."
